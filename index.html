    What this checker doesn’t look at

    • Whether your sources are real or described correctly. It only checks how each reference is formatted.
    • Figures, images, and the fine details of tables.
    • Length, quality, grammar, tone, or word choice, including bias-free language.
    • For discussion posts, page layout is not checked at all (margins, fonts, spacing, indents, title page, page numbers, and the hanging indent on references), because a discussion board decides how the post looks.
    • Anything the automatic fixes can’t do safely, such as missing citations, title page details, page numbers, or the capital letters in your headings. Those are marked “Needs you”. Look over a fixed copy in Word or Google Docs before you hand it in.
    • Special rules from your teacher or journal. If they ask for something different from standard APA 7, follow them. Look over every flagged item yourself, because the checker can miss things or get things wrong.

    Checks follow the APA 7th edition Publication Manual. Word and Google Docs files (.docx) only.
